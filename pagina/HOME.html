Featured artist

The Raux Academy selects approximately 700 distinct pieces of contemporary art for display in their collective exhibit. Nine individuals are granted six of her own exhibit hall to display entire collections or themed pieces. Each Featured Artist has an opportunity to speak at the conference to share his or her vision, perspective, and techniques with conference attendees.

The venue

Hotel Contemp

CAC spanning events and gallery activities take place in the Hotel Contemp at 300 1st Avenue. Overlooking downtown, this Art Deco treasure features modern touches to meet every need, and a sampling of restaurants and nightlife makes the venue a much sought after event location and conference room.

Hotel Contemp is the perfect spot for a gathering of modern artists or any group that enjoy contemporary art and sculpture. But the intimate guest artists know their events can be quite exclusive from the Roux Monroe Purple suite (filled with wall unit paintings) to the Torii Lounge Sculpted Universe suite, with dozens of original sculptures.

Visitors are sure to be intrigued and comforted during their stay at Hotel Contemp.
