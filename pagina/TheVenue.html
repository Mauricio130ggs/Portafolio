The Venue

Hotel Contempo is the perfect spot for a gathering of modern artists. Not only are the conference rooms inspiring with breathtaking contemporary art and sculptures, but the individual rooms are as unique as the renowned artists who were commissioned to decorate them. From the Plaza Monoae Purple penthouse to the eclectic Wanderer's Delight, each palette of vivid and lavender to the Tea Lounge Sculpted Universal with rusty yellows of golden bronze with warm highlights, the guest rooms are likely to be the next talent. Visitors are sure to be intrigued and comforted during their four and a half day conference while Hotel Contempo.
