Tuesday March 8, 2016
Water in Art
Water in Art is the theme for the second day, as art students from around the world gather at the Fountain of Intrigue in the garden of Hotel Conference to catch up on contemporary and traditional techniques in capturing water as an art material, and water as an art subject.
Water in Art Kickoff Session
09:30-10:30am
Elizabeth Hall
Jennifer Jerome, a native of New Orleans, whose work has centered around abstract images that depict flooding and rebuilding, will talk about how the floods inspired and influenced her unique artistic style of rebirth after devastation and lives lost, her work also depicts the hope and togetherness of a community that has persevered.
Ice Sculptures
10:30am-1pm
Fountain Of Intrigue
Get out your mittens and earmuffs, and join your fellow artists at the Fountain of Intrigue, in the Hotel Conference Building, where the ambient temperature has been turned down to allow the sculpting of ice into the most mysterious and beautiful of shapes. Richard Reed will share his secrets for chiseling ice into a shape that your imagination has envisioned. There is an extra fee of $25 for the rental of the tools needed to sculpt so, if you plan to participate, find me here you do!
